Imereti

Imereti is a beautiful historic region in western Georgia. Its main city, Kutaisi, is very old and famous for its ancient buildings and green hills. Many tourists visit Imereti to see wonderful nature, like large caves and green forests. The region is also famous for its delicious food, especially Imeretian khachapuri (a traditional flatbread filled with cheese). The people who live there are very friendly and always welcome guests with warm hospitality. It is a fantastic place to travel if you want to see history, nature, and enjoy great food.
